      About

      Each lesson has 10 sentences with one word missing. Pick the word that fits. The translation underneath helps when the sentence alone isn’t enough.

      A lesson mixes up to 8 reviews with at least 2 new sentences. Each time you get a sentence right, it waits one more day before coming back: 1 day, then 2, then 3. If you get it wrong, it returns in your next lesson.

      Sentences

      All sentences and translations come from Tatoeba, a collection of sentences written and translated by volunteers. They are licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR.

      Few sentences are translated directly between Swedish and Romanian. Most pairs are linked through a shared English translation, so a hint can be a looser match than a direct translation.
